**Free-tier principle:** everything that runs on the user's machine with
user-supplied resources (BYOK keys, local models, free tools) is FREE. Paid
tiers sell coordination (teams, fleet, centralized control/audit) and managed
infrastructure (GPU, managed AI) — never basic safety.
